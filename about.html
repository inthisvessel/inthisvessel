Background

Every role I've held has been about the same thing: seeing who the system leaves behind and figuring out why.

From coordinating patient referrals at a community health clinic to working the fraud lines at a bank, I've spent years in the spaces where systems meet real people.

I'm a psychology-trained product designer finishing a B.S. at Kennesaw State. My psychology background isn't adjacent to design. It's the foundation of how I design. I don't start with screens. I start with behavior, then build the system that moves the outcome.
